AI that runs in production, not in pilots.

H4 Global Advisors helps mid-market leadership teams turn AI ambition into working operations: the right use cases, the operating model to run them, and the delivery leadership to get them live.

Email us See how we work

Most companies have tried AI. Far fewer have it running part of the business.

The gap is rarely the technology. It is ownership, priorities, data, process change, and someone accountable for getting it over the line. Mid-market teams feel this most: the ambition matches the enterprise, but without a dedicated transformation office to carry it. That is the gap we fill.

What we do

Three ways we work with leadership teams, on their own or together.

  • AI readiness and roadmap

    Find where AI will actually pay off in your business, rank the use cases by value and effort, and build a plan and business case your leadership team can commit to.

  • Operating model and governance

    Set ownership, decision rights, guardrails for responsible use, and the measures that show whether AI is delivering. The structure that keeps it working after launch.

  • Delivery leadership

    We stand up and run the program alongside your team: vendors, priorities, milestones, and adoption, until the capability is live and owned internally.

How an engagement runs

Every engagement moves in one direction: from a promising idea to something your team runs without us.

PilotProduction

  1. Assess

    Understand your goals, data, systems, and team readiness. Agree on where to start.

  2. Design

    Shape the use cases, operating model, and success measures around how your business actually works.

  3. Deliver

    Lead execution with your team and vendors, with clear milestones and regular leadership reviews.

  4. Hand off

    Transfer ownership, documentation, and know-how so the capability keeps running and improving.
